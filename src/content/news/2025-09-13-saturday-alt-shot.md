title: Four up, and a half to show for it
summary: Blue take the Saturday alt shot 4½–1½ and lead by four going into the singles, and Red's only win is the heaviest of the cup.
published_at: 2025-09-13T18:00
cup: 2025
---

Four up with ten to play, Rohan Lall and Ryan Zaporzan came off the eighteenth green with half a
point. Mike Kalic and Stu Shiells had trailed from the third hole and did not lead the match at any
stage of it, which makes winning the last to halve it less a comeback than a robbery.

Red's only win of the morning was the heaviest beating of the cup so far. Harman Kaler and Connor
Macaulay were five up on Keith Van Walleghem and Brad Zander inside five holes, six up by the tenth,
and closed it out 6 & 5 on the thirteenth. Zander lost on Friday morning, won on Friday afternoon,
and has now been beaten by more holes than anyone this week.

Nigel Milnes won a match. Alongside Jon Ray he was level with Garry Hargreaves and Erik St-Hilaire
after eleven holes, and then took the twelfth, thirteenth, fourteenth and fifteenth without reply to
finish it 4 & 3. The Blue captain's first point of the week arrives at the third time of asking.
Hargreaves has still not managed one at all.

The two men who captained last year's cup played it out together and lost by a hole. Travis Bale and
Evan Martin, who between them settled 2024 by a single point, led Jason Gunnlaugson and Iain McDonald
exactly once all morning — after the ninth — were level again at the seventeenth, and lost the
eighteenth.

Blair Horn and Justin Rabe took five of the first seven holes from Matt Phin and Adam Sefton and were
never pressed after it, winning 5 & 3. That is three matches and three defeats for Phin, who has now
contributed two holes to Red's week and nothing else.

Ian Fordyce and Matt Pilloud were level with Harbs Benning and Dusty McMillan for the last time at
the third, went three up by the sixth, and spent the next ten holes declining to give any of it back.
3 & 2.

**Blue 4½, Red 1½ on the morning, and Blue 11 – Red 7 overall.** Only the twelve singles are left and
fifteen and a half wins the cup, which puts Blue four and a half away and Red eight and a half. They
go out this afternoon.
