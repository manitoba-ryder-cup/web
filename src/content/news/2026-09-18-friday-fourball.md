Connor Macaulay and Matt Phin were five up on the Red captain after eleven holes and came off the
eighteenth with half a point. Harbs Benning and Keith Van Walleghem won the thirteenth, the
fourteenth, the fifteenth, the sixteenth and the seventeenth — five in a row, four of them on
Benning's ball alone — and halved the last to halve the match. Phin lost all four of his matches
last year; on this evidence he has learned to lose only half of one.

Every other match had a winner, and Blue took three of the five.

Jon Ray's first match as Blue captain was decided on the eighteenth, and by Ray. Level with Evan
Martin and Dan McInnes after seventeen, he and Ian Fordyce took the last to win by a hole. It was
Ray who won the twelfth too, in a match that never got further apart than one either way. McInnes
did not win a hole on his own all morning.

Garry Hargreaves and Justin Rabe make an odd pair. One went four matches without a win last year
and the other four without a loss, and Blue's draft put them on the same card. They were four up
after five on Blair Horn and Matt Pilloud, every one of those holes Rabe's, and then Pilloud took
over: five holes on his own, the last of them the eighteenth, and Red won it two up. Hargreaves
did not win a hole. Neither, for that matter, did Horn.

Mike Kalic and Dustin Mansfield were seven up after twelve on Brad Zander and Ryan Zaporzan, which
is where the match ended. 7 & 6. Zaporzan won the second and that was Red's morning; Zander
did not win a hole.

Aaron Challis and Rohan Lall never trailed Trevor Horn and Iain McDonald. Lall won four holes
outright, including the last two, and it finished 5 & 3.

Red's one clean win was Travis Bale's. All square through seven with Stu Shiells against Dusty
McMillan and Nigel Milnes, Bale then won the eighth, tenth, eleventh, twelfth and thirteenth
outright — five of the next six — and it was over on the fourteenth, 5 & 4. Milnes won nothing on
his own; he captained Blue to the cup last year and has now lost four of his last five matches.

**Blue 3½, Red 2½.** Twenty-four of the thirty points are still to play for and fifteen and a half
wins it. Alt shot is out this afternoon.
