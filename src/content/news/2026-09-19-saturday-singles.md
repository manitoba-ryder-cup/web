Blue came to the singles a point ahead and needing six of the twelve. They got six and a half, and
the cup, 16–14.

The captain took his on the eighteenth green. Jon Ray was four down to Matt Pilloud after ten holes.
He won the eleventh, the thirteenth, and then the fifteenth, sixteenth, seventeenth and eighteenth —
four in a row to finish — and took the match by a hole having never led it until the last putt. Ray
lost two of his three with a partner this week; on his own, with the cup on the table, he won.

Nigel Milnes and Trevor Horn halved the first. They halved the second. They halved all eighteen,
which no match in two cups has done, and each side took half a point from a match nobody won a hole
of. Milnes ends the week with three defeats and a half; so does Horn.

Red's best golf came too late to matter, and a great deal of it came from Evan Martin. Eight up
after nine on Ian Fordyce and finished on the eleventh, 8 & 7 — three holes wider than anything in
last year's cup. Harbs Benning did something nearly as unlikely from the other direction: five down
to Justin Rabe after eight, he won six of the last nine and took it 2 & 1. Rabe was five up with ten
to play and lost.

The rest of Red's five were Travis Bale over Matt Phin 3 & 2, never behind; Keith Van Walleghem over
Garry Hargreaves 5 & 3; and Ryan Zaporzan over Rohan Lall 3 & 2 — Lall's only defeat of the week,
having been three up after five.

Blue's other five were routine by comparison. Mike Kalic beat Stu Shiells 4 & 3, Shiells's only
loss. Dusty McMillan beat Dan McInnes 5 & 3, six up after nine. Connor Macaulay came from two down
to beat Blair Horn 3 & 1. Aaron Challis beat Iain McDonald 2 & 1 to finish the week four from four,
the only man to win every match he played. Dustin Mansfield beat Brad Zander two up, having trailed
after eight.

**Blue 16, Red 14.** One point apart after Friday, one apart after Saturday morning, and the last
session decided by a half. Jon Ray's first cup as captain is a winning one; Harbs Benning's is not,
though he ends it having won his last two.
