---
title: Seventeen halves and then a winner
summary: The Friday scotch is split 3–3, one match is halved on every hole but the last, and Red cut Blue's lead to a point.
published_at: 2026-09-18T23:00
cup: 2026
---

Dan McInnes and Stu Shiells halved the first hole with Ian Fordyce and Dusty McMillan. Then they
halved the second. They halved every hole to the seventeenth, eighteen holes without either side
ever being ahead, and on the last one Red won it. One up, and not a single hole of it decided until
the final green. No match in the last two cups has been halved on that many holes in a row.

That was the afternoon's closest, and the rest of it was not far behind: three of the six went to
the eighteenth and two more to the sixteenth.

Blair Horn and Brad Zander were six up after eight on Dustin Mansfield and Matt Phin, and then lost
five of the next eight to be one up with two to play. Red won the seventeenth to close it 2 & 1 —
which is a win, but a six-hole lead that ends one hole clear is not the shape a win is meant to be.
Horn and Zander both got round the morning without winning a hole on their own; between them they
won seven of the first eight here.

The two captains went out in the second match, on opposite sides, and neither of them won it. Harbs
Benning and Travis Bale trailed Aaron Challis and Justin Rabe from the second hole and were never in
front. They squared it four times — at the fourth, eighth, tenth and twelfth — and could not get in
front once. Blue won the thirteenth and held it. The Blue captain had a better afternoon. Jon Ray
and Nigel Milnes fell two behind Iain McDonald and Keith Van Walleghem by the third and never got
closer than one, losing 3 & 2 — Milnes is two from two in defeat, and has lost five of his last six
across two cups.

Mike Kalic and Connor Macaulay beat Trevor Horn and Evan Martin 5 & 3, five up from the eleventh on
and no closer than two after the eighth. Kalic has now won both of his matches and been on the
course for a total of twenty-seven holes.

Garry Hargreaves and Rohan Lall never trailed Matt Pilloud and Ryan Zaporzan, went four up after
fourteen, and gave three of those back on the way to the house to win by two. Lall has two wins from
two; Hargreaves, who did not win a hole on his own this morning, has now won a match.

**Blue 6½, Red 5½.** Eighteen of the thirty points remain and fifteen and a half wins it. Alt shot
is out first thing tomorrow, and singles decide it in the afternoon.
