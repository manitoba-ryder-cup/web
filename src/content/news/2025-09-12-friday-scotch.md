---
title: The two who won nothing win by four
summary: Red take the Friday afternoon scotch 3½–2½, Nigel Milnes loses twice in a day, and the first half of the cup arrives on the eighteenth.
published_at: 2025-09-12T20:00
cup: 2025
---

Travis Bale and Connor Macaulay went out in separate matches in the morning and won nothing between
them. Put together in the afternoon they took Blair Horn and Iain McDonald apart, three up inside
eight holes and home 4 & 2. Whatever fourball asks of a player, neither of them seems to have it.

Nigel Milnes lost again. Harbs Benning and Erik St-Hilaire, both beaten before lunch themselves, put
the Blue captain and Matt Pilloud away 3 & 2 in a match that was all square four times inside eight
holes and never level again after it. Two matches, two defeats, and the captaincy has had better
opening days.

Evan Martin and Ryan Zaporzan spent the rest of the afternoon giving a lead away and getting it back.
Four up after ten on Ian Fordyce and Mike Kalic, they lost the thirteenth, the fifteenth, the
sixteenth and the seventeenth to arrive at the last all square, having led the match for sixteen
holes and led nothing at the only point it mattered. Then they won the eighteenth. Red's narrowest
point of the day and the one they will least want to talk about.

The first match out was the one that would not finish. Jon Ray and Stu Shiells were two up on Rohan
Lall and Adam Sefton at the turn and still one up standing on the eighteenth tee, and lost the hole.
A half each, and the first of the cup.

Justin Rabe and Brad Zander did not trail Garry Hargreaves and Harman Kaler at any stage. Two up
after three, three up after eleven, and although Red took the thirteenth back the last four holes
were all halved and it ended 2 & 1. Hargreaves has now lost twice in a day, having led one of them by
four with thirteen to play. Zander, who has a long-standing habit of turning up, doing the job and
thinking no more about golf until the following September, has two-thirds of that seen to already.

Jason Gunnlaugson and Keith Van Walleghem were pegged level at the sixth by Dusty McMillan and Matt
Phin and then won six of the next eight to finish it 5 & 3. Van Walleghem took both his matches on
Friday. Phin lost both, which is hard on a man who was the only Red player to win a hole in the
morning's heaviest defeat.

**Red 3½, Blue 2½ on the afternoon, and Blue 6½ – Red 5½ on the day.** Eighteen of the thirty points
are still to play for and fifteen and a half wins the cup, so a full day of golf has separated these
two by one point. Alt shot goes out first thing tomorrow.
