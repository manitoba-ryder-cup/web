---
title: Four men, four matches, not a hole
summary: Blue win the opening fourball 4-2, and four players get round a whole match without winning a hole on their own.
published_at: 2025-09-12T13:00
cup: 2025
---

Twelve months ago Travis Bale captained Red to within a single point of the cup. He opened this one
by going thirteen holes without winning any of them. Matt Phin took the third and the tenth and that
was the whole of Red's contribution: Jon Ray won five outright, Ian Fordyce two more, and it was
over by the thirteenth. 7 & 5. Ray has spent several Ryder Cups failing to close out tight matches,
and this one was never close enough to ask.

He was not the only one to come off empty-handed. Connor Macaulay, Blue captain Nigel Milnes and
Mike Kalic all played out their matches without winning a hole on their own. Four men, four matches,
not a hole between them.

Four up inside five holes, Garry Hargreaves and Adam Sefton then did not win another until the
sixteenth, by which point Blair Horn and Matt Pilloud had taken six of seven and the match with
them. Hargreaves went through last year's cup without a win in four matches, and has now found a
more inventive way to lose one.

The same arithmetic nearly undid Blue an hour earlier. Justin Rabe and Keith Van Walleghem were four
up after ten on Harbs Benning and Macaulay, then handed back the twelfth, thirteenth, fourteenth and
fifteenth in a row to be pegged level — two of those four to Benning alone. They took the
seventeenth between them and got out one up.

Milnes was finished three holes earlier than that. Evan Martin, who captained the winning side a
year ago and by a single point, took the ninth, fourteenth and fifteenth outright, with Dusty
McMillan adding the third and the fourth. Four straight from the third put it beyond reach and they
closed it 5 & 3.

Red's other point was the hardest earned of the morning, and the only one settled on the last.
Harman Kaler and Rohan Lall were two up on Stu Shiells and Brad Zander after four and pegged back
level by the seventh. Two up again after twelve, level again by the fifteenth. That left Shiells and
Zander three holes to win a match they had twice hauled back, and they took none of them — halved
the sixteenth, halved the seventeenth, and lost the eighteenth to Lall, who won it on his own.

The lead changed hands seven times in the last match out, and two men decided all of it. Iain
McDonald won the fourth, fifth, sixth, eleventh and sixteenth outright for Blue and shared the
fourteenth; Kalic did not win a single hole on his own all morning. Erik St-Hilaire was carrying Red
just as squarely — the seventh, the eighth and the seventeenth were his alone. Two up after two, one
down by the sixth, level again at the eleventh, Red came to the last needing it to halve the match.
They halved the hole instead.

**Blue 4, Red 2.** Twenty-four of the thirty points are still out there and fifteen and a half wins
the cup, so two down after one session is a deficit rather than a problem. Scotch is out this
afternoon.
